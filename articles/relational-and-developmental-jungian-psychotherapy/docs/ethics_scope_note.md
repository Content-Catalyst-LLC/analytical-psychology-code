# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- clinical decision-making;
- treatment recommendation;
- therapist evaluation;
- supervision judgment;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about relational Jungian psychotherapy, attachment, affect regulation, rupture-and-repair, symbolic capacity, shame, fragmentation, and developmental integration, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, or therapeutic relationship.
