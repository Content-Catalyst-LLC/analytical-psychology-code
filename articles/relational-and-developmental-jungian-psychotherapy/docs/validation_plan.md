# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about psychotherapy process, therapeutic effectiveness, clinical outcomes, therapist performance, or patient change.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate clinical interpretation, research measurement, and conceptual modeling.
3. Use validated psychotherapy-process and outcome measures where appropriate.
4. Work with qualified clinicians and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
5. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that purpose.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable clinical material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include therapist, patient, cultural, and institutional context when interpreting process data.
8. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
9. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
10. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not clinical truth.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian therapy, diagnose patients, recommend treatment, evaluate therapists, predict treatment outcomes, or replace clinical judgment. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
