# Relational Jungian Clinical Concepts Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a treatment protocol.

## Relational field

- What is happening between patient and analyst, not only inside the patient?
- Is the analyst treating transference as projection only, or as a living developmental field?
- What is the analyst feeling, and how might that belong to the field?
- Is rupture being recognized and repaired, or interpreted defensively?

## Developmental capacity

- Can the patient use interpretation, or is containment needed first?
- Is symbolic material deepening integration or overwhelming the patient?
- Is shame suppressing dream, play, affect, or imagination?
- Is developmental injury appearing as fragmentation, deadness, or defensive autonomy?

## Symbol and dream

- Does the dream express archetypal meaning, developmental history, field process, or all of these?
- Is amplification serving the patient’s process or bypassing affect?
- Is active imagination safe enough for this patient’s ego and regulatory capacity?
- Is symbolic sophistication masking dissociation or shame?

## Ethics and power

- Are boundaries clear, reliable, and emotionally intelligible?
- Is the analyst’s subjectivity disciplined by ethics, supervision, and reflection?
- Is self-disclosure serving the patient or the analyst?
- Are cultural, social, and institutional power dynamics being taken seriously?

## Integration

- Is relational safety strengthening symbolic capacity?
- Is repair capacity increasing after rupture?
- Is fragmentation decreasing without forcing premature unity?
- Is individuation becoming more embodied, relational, and humane?
