# Ethics and Scope Note

These files should not be used for diagnosis, therapy, mental-health evaluation, gender assessment, sexuality assessment, clinical decision-making, treatment recommendation, crisis intervention, interpretation of private dreams or therapy material, employment selection, hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, termination, workplace surveillance, individual performance management, or individual profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about projection, symbolic otherness, gender-code rigidity, reflective mediation, symbolic flexibility, and integration, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, personal context, or symbolic humility.
