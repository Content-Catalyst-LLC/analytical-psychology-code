# Data Dictionary

The datasets are synthetic and illustrative. They do not represent real people, therapy records, clinical material, dream records, gender assessments, sexuality assessments, mental-health assessments, employment records, or empirical findings.

## Main variables

- `unrealized_capacity`: stylized disowned or unrealized psychic capacity.
- `affective_charge`: stylized emotional intensity or salience.
- `relational_trigger`: stylized relational cue activating projection.
- `reflective_mediation`: stylized capacity to reflect on projection and differentiate symbol from person.
- `gender_code_rigidity`: stylized rigidity of historically learned gender coding.
- `symbolic_flexibility`: stylized flexibility in symbolic interpretation and identity coding.
- `symbolic_discrepancy`: stylized distance between integrated capacity and externalized symbolic capacity.
- `projection_intensity`: synthetic projection intensity score.
- `projection_rigidity`: synthetic rigidity of projection score.
- `symbolic_integration`: synthetic symbolic integration score.

These variables are conceptual demonstrations, not validated psychological, clinical, diagnostic, predictive, gender, sexuality, dream-interpretive, or assessment measures.
