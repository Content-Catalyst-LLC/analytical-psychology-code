# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. Any empirical or clinical extension would require careful construct definition, ethical review, consent, privacy protection, trauma-informed safeguards, cultural and gender-theoretical review, and comparison with object relations, attachment theory, cognitive schemas, gender studies, queer theory, affect theory, and relational psychoanalysis.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not diagnose people, interpret real dreams, assess gender, assess sexuality, recommend treatment, replace clinical judgment, or prove analytical psychology.
