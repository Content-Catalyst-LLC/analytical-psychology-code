# Symbolic Otherness Checklist

- What figure appears, and what does it do?
- What affect does the figure carry?
- Is an actual other being overinvested with symbolic meaning?
- What capacity, fear, desire, authority, vulnerability, or longing is being externalized?
- Is the image gendered? Who or what has coded this quality as gendered?
- Is interpretation naturalizing a stereotype?
- Does the symbolic figure exceed binary categories?
- Is the framework respecting queer, trans, and nonbinary experience?
- Is the actual person being differentiated from the projected image?
- Is the conclusion provisional, contextual, humble, and ethically grounded?
