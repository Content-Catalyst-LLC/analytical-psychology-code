# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about shadow dynamics, projection, persona rigidity, reflective capacity, shame, integration, therapy, diagnosis, morality, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, moral philosophy, social analysis, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat model outputs as measurements of real shadow, guilt, projection, morality, or individuation.
4. Use synthetic, public-domain, or ethically approved materials only.
5. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, statisticians, historians of psychology, ethics scholars, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, clinical records, dream records, relationship records, workplace records, or private life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
8. Compare Jungian interpretation with alternative explanations, including cognitive dissonance, moral psychology, affect regulation, attachment, object relations, social identity, trauma studies, and institutional analysis.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Pair computational findings with close reading, clinical/contextual judgment, ethical accountability, and concrete social analysis where relevant.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not diagnose people, interpret real dreams, rank morality, recommend treatment, replace clinical judgment, or prove analytical psychology. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
