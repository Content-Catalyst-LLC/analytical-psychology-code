# Shadow Work Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, moral ranking system, or individuation-measurement service.

## Recognition

- What is being strongly condemned, feared, idealized, or repeatedly encountered?
- Is the affect proportional to the situation?
- Is the reaction repetitive across relationships or contexts?
- What dream figures, doubles, pursuers, hidden rooms, animals, or threatening figures appear?

## Persona and identity

- What conscious self-image is being protected?
- What would threaten the persona?
- What does the person or group need to believe about itself?
- What qualities cannot be admitted without shame or identity threat?

## Projection

- What belongs to the actual other?
- What belongs to the relationship?
- What may belong to the disowned life of the self?
- Is judgment serving truth, self-protection, or both?

## Integration

- Can the material be named without enactment?
- Can the person distinguish impulse, affect, image, fantasy, and ethical action?
- Is reflection leading to changed conduct or only self-analysis?
- Is repair or accountability required?

## Collective shadow

- What official identity does the group maintain?
- What harm, aggression, dependency, corruption, or contradiction is disowned?
- Who is made to carry the projection?
- What material analysis is needed alongside psychological interpretation?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not treat shadow as a slogan.
- Do not reduce real harm to projection.
- Do not use shadow language to silence criticism.
- Do not confuse shame with responsibility.
- Keep interpretation provisional, contextual, accountable, and ethically grounded.
