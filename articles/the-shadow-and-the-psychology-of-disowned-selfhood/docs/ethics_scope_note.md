# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- mental-health evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- crisis intervention;
- clinical decision-making;
- moral ranking;
- proof of guilt, innocence, maturity, projection, shadow integration, or individuation;
- interpretation of private dream journals;
- interpretation of therapy material;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about shadow discrepancy, projection, persona rigidity, reflective capacity, shame response, integration, and responsibility, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, personal context, social analysis, or accountability.
