# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- spiritual direction;
- treatment recommendation;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about Jungian alchemy, symbolic transformation, alchemical stages, opposition, containment, and integration, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, historical scholarship, ethical interpretation, empirical validation, or therapeutic care.
