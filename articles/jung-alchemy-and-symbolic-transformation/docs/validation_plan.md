# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical, historical, or textual extension would require careful validation before making any claim about real psychotherapy, alchemical texts, symbolic transformation, dream sequences, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate historical alchemy from Jungian psychological interpretation.
3. Document which alchemical corpus or edition is being analyzed.
4. Distinguish symbolic amplification from historical reconstruction.
5. Use human-coded textual annotation before automating symbolic analysis.
6. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated research instrument exists.
7. Include disconfirming cases and alternative interpretations.
8. Include historian-of-science and religious-studies perspectives.
9. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
10. Use clinical material only with consent, anonymization, and ethical safeguards.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate alchemy, prove Jung’s interpretation, diagnose patients, recommend therapy, assign spiritual stages, or rank symbolic development. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
