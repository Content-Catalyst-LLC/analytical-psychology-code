# Alchemical Interpretation Checklist

Use this checklist when applying Jungian alchemical interpretation.

## Symbolic process

- What stage or operation appears: nigredo, albedo, rubedo, coniunctio, dissolution, separation, coagulation, vessel, Mercurius, stone?
- Is the symbol part of a sequence or a single image?
- What is transforming, and what resists transformation?
- Is the process contained, overheated, scattered, or prematurely fixed?

## Psychological caution

- Is darkness being romanticized?
- Is crisis being mistaken for transformation without enough containment?
- Is the vessel strong enough for the symbolic heat?
- Is the language being used to bypass clinical or practical care?

## Historical caution

- Is historical alchemy being reduced to psychology?
- Are material, medicinal, theological, laboratory, and cosmological dimensions being preserved?
- Is Jung’s reading being distinguished from historical reconstruction?
- Is the interpretation presented as an amplification rather than a final explanation?

## Interpretive discipline

- Does the image itself support the reading?
- Are alternative symbolic, historical, religious, or textual meanings considered?
- Does the interpretation preserve matter as well as psyche?
- Does the conclusion avoid overclaiming?
