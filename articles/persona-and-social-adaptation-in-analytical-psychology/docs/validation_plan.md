# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about persona, role identity, burnout, psychic strain, professional identity, digital visibility, therapy, diagnosis, employment, reputation, social scoring, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, organizational psychology, social psychology, sociology, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat model outputs as measurements of real persona strength, role adequacy, burnout, mental health, reputation, personality, employability, or individuation.
4. Use synthetic, public-domain, or ethically approved materials only.
5. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, organizational psychologists, sociologists, statisticians, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, clinical records, employment records, HR files, social-media records, reputation data, relationship records, or private life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
8. Compare Jungian interpretation with alternative explanations, including role theory, impression management, identity formation, false-self theory, burnout research, social identity theory, organizational psychology, and institutional analysis.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Pair computational findings with close reading, clinical/contextual judgment, ethical accountability, and social analysis where relevant.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not diagnose people, assess personality, evaluate employees, rank reputations, recommend treatment, replace clinical judgment, or prove analytical psychology. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
