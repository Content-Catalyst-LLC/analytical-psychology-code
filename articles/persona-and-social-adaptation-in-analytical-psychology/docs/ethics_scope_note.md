# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- mental-health evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- crisis intervention;
- clinical decision-making;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- reputation scoring;
- social scoring;
- personality assessment;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about persona strength, persona rigidity, role demand, public recognition, institutional reward, inward complexity, psychic strain, burnout risk, shadow compensation, and individuation readiness, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, personal context, social analysis, or accountability.
