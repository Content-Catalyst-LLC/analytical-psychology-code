# Persona Analysis Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, employment assessment, reputation-scoring system, social-scoring system, or individuation-measurement service.

## Persona recognition

- What role is being performed?
- What audience recognizes or rewards the role?
- What institution, family, profession, or platform reinforces the role?
- What public qualities are emphasized?

## Flexibility

- Can the person step back from the role?
- Can the role change across contexts?
- Can failure, uncertainty, or vulnerability be admitted?
- Does the person know who they are when the role is not active?

## Shadow and exclusion

- What qualities are excluded by the persona?
- What emotions, needs, desires, fears, or conflicts cannot be admitted?
- Where does shadow pressure appear?
- What does the person condemn or envy in others?

## Institutions and power

- Who has the freedom to relax the persona?
- Who must maintain persona for safety or survival?
- What institutional rewards reinforce the role?
- What cannot be said without penalty?

## Digital persona

- What metrics reinforce the identity?
- What expressions are rewarded or punished by the audience?
- Does the person remain able to change privately?
- Has the profile become more stable than the person?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not treat persona as fake by default.
- Do not romanticize unfiltered inwardness.
- Do not use persona language for employment screening or reputation scoring.
- Do not ignore power and structural vulnerability.
- Keep interpretation provisional, contextual, accountable, and ethically grounded.
