# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- crisis assessment;
- suicide or self-harm risk prediction;
- psychological assessment;
- clinical decision-making;
- treatment recommendation;
- therapist evaluation;
- supervision judgment;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about trauma, dissociation, affect intensity, ego integration, symbolic capacity, relational safety, bodily regulation, nightmare intrusion, memory continuity, symbolic recovery, and integration potential, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, or therapeutic relationship.
