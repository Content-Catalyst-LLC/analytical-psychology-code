# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about personality, typology, complexes, shadow, psychological development, personality assessment, diagnosis, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, research measurement, and conceptual modeling.
3. Use validated personality, identity, attachment, symptom, and well-being measures where appropriate.
4. Avoid treating Jungian typology as a hiring, assessment, or clinical decision tool.
5. Work with qualified personality psychologists, clinicians, statisticians, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that specific purpose.
7. Protect privacy and never use identifiable clinical, personal, employment, dream, therapy, or life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
8. Include cultural, gendered, socioeconomic, institutional, religious, and historical context.
9. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
10. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian personality theory, diagnose people, classify personality types, recommend treatment, support employment decisions, predict development, or replace clinical judgment. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
