# Personality Theory Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a personality assessment, diagnostic tool, screening tool, or treatment protocol.

## Personality structure

- What is the person’s conscious orientation or typological style?
- What role does persona play in social adaptation?
- What traits, functions, values, or roles are overdeveloped?
- What capacities appear underdeveloped, disowned, or projected?

## Shadow and complexes

- What complex appears to organize repeated reactions?
- What does the ego reject from conscious self-description?
- Is the shadow being projected onto others?
- Is the pattern being interpreted carefully rather than used as a label?

## Typology and development

- Is typology being used as inquiry rather than fixed identity?
- What inferior or neglected function carries developmental pressure?
- Is the person being reduced to a type label?
- Are traits and Jungian functions being distinguished clearly?

## Symbol and personality

- What recurring dreams, images, motifs, or life patterns clarify the personality?
- Does symbolic interpretation remain grounded in actual biography, body, culture, and relationship?
- Is symbolic language clarifying the person or flattering the ego?
- Is unconscious compensation being interpreted without overclaiming?

## Ethics and use

- Is this material being kept away from hiring, screening, surveillance, or individual evaluation?
- Are cultural, historical, gendered, racialized, institutional, and social conditions being considered?
- Is Jungian depth being used to deepen understanding rather than replace evidence?
- Does the analysis increase humility, responsibility, and interpretive care?
