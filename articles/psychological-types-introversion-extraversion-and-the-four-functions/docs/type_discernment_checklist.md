# Psychological Type Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a personality assessment, type test, diagnostic tool, screening tool, or treatment protocol.

## Orientation and function

- What function appears most differentiated in conscious life?
- What attitude appears most trusted: introverted orientation or extraverted orientation?
- Is the person being reduced to a type label?
- Is type being used as inquiry rather than identity?

## Inferior function

- What function appears least differentiated?
- Does the inferior function appear through mood, awkwardness, projection, dreams, attraction, or conflict?
- Is the inferior function being dismissed, inflated, or approached carefully?
- What developmental invitation may be hidden in the inferior function?

## One-sidedness and compensation

- What strength has become too exclusive?
- What does the dominant function allow the person to do well?
- What does it leave underdeveloped?
- Where is unconscious compensation appearing?

## Culture and institution

- What functions does the surrounding culture, family, school, workplace, or institution reward?
- What functions does it shame, punish, or ignore?
- Could apparent type be persona or adaptation?
- Are social power, safety, class, gender, race, disability, and institutional context being considered?

## Ethics and use

- Is typology being kept away from hiring, screening, surveillance, and individual evaluation?
- Does the analysis increase humility rather than self-branding?
- Does the analysis avoid treating type as fate?
- Does the work point toward development rather than confirmation of a fixed identity?
