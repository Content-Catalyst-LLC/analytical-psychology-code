# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- treatment recommendation;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about post-Jungian clinical development, symbolic depth, attachment, trauma, embodiment, cultural responsiveness, and clinical adequacy, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, lived context, ethical interpretation, empirical validation, trauma-informed care, or therapeutic containment.
