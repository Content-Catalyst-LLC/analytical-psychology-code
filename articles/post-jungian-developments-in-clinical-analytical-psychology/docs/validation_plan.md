# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about real psychotherapy, clinical outcomes, analytical psychology, trauma treatment, attachment processes, or analyst practice.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate clinical interpretation from empirical claim.
3. Use validated measures where appropriate.
4. Review Jungian, psychoanalytic, psychodynamic, attachment, trauma, and psychotherapy outcome research.
5. Obtain ethical approval for human-subjects research.
6. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that purpose.
7. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
8. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
9. Include clinical containment, risk, culture, and patient-readiness variables.
10. Use case material only with consent, anonymization, and ethical safeguards.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate analytical psychology, post-Jungian clinical practice, dream interpretation, trauma work, attachment-informed psychotherapy, or any treatment model. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
