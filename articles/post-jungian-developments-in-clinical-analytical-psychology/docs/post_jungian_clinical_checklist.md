# Post-Jungian Clinical Method Checklist

Use this checklist when thinking conceptually about post-Jungian clinical interpretation.

## Symbolic depth

- What dream, symbol, complex, image, or archetypal pattern is active?
- Is the unconscious compensating a one-sided conscious position?
- Does the material invite amplification, or is amplification premature?
- Is the symbol clinically usable by this patient now?

## Development and attachment

- What early relational pattern may be active?
- Is the patient able to mentalize, symbolize, and tolerate affect?
- Does the presentation involve primitive anxiety, shame, or fragile selfhood?
- What kind of holding or repair is needed?

## Trauma and dissociation

- Is there acute risk, dissociation, fragmentation, shutdown, or hyperarousal?
- Does the material require stabilization before interpretation?
- Is the image symbolic, traumatic, dissociated, or all three?
- Could archetypal interpretation overwhelm or bypass the patient?

## Relational field

- What is happening in transference and countertransference?
- Is an enactment unfolding?
- What does the analyst feel, and how should that be reflected rather than acted out?
- Has rupture and repair become part of the work?

## Embodiment and culture

- What bodily states are present?
- What social, cultural, racial, gendered, religious, or institutional context shapes the material?
- Is symbolic interpretation preserving or erasing history and power?
- What does ethical clinical responsibility require before interpretation?
