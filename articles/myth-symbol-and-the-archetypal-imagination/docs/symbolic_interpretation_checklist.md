# Symbolic Interpretation Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review.

## Recurrence and difference

- What symbolic motif appears?
- Does the motif recur across multiple source types or contexts?
- What is similar across appearances?
- What is different across appearances?
- What historical, ritual, linguistic, political, religious, ecological, or literary context changes the meaning?

## Archetypal caution

- Is the archetypal claim necessary, or would genre, borrowing, ecology, history, or politics explain the recurrence better?
- Is the image being treated as a fixed meaning rather than a symbolic field?
- Is the interpretation turning resemblance into sameness?
- Is the symbol being extracted from a living tradition without respect for its own meaning?

## Power and ethics

- Who uses the symbol?
- Who benefits from this mythic structure?
- Who is endangered, silenced, demonized, or erased?
- Does the symbol support memory, repair, justice, and humility, or does it justify domination, purity, exclusion, or violence?

## Method

- Are motif dictionary choices documented?
- Are public-domain or ethically approved texts used?
- Are computational outputs paired with close reading?
- Are alternative explanations considered?
- Is uncertainty stated clearly?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not claim proof of archetypes from motif recurrence alone.
- Do not flatten religious or cultural specificity.
- Do not rank cultures.
- Do not automate sacred interpretation.
- Do not replace historical scholarship with symbolic enthusiasm.
