# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- cultural ranking;
- religious evaluation;
- individual psychological profiling.

Critique modeling can clarify assumptions about evidence, culture, universality, gender, race, coloniality, and overgeneralization, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, lived context, empirical validation, tradition-specific scholarship, or ethical interpretation.
