# Jungian Critique Checklist

Use this checklist when evaluating a Jungian concept or interpretive claim.

## Evidence

- Is the claim empirical, clinical, hermeneutic, phenomenological, philosophical, or metaphorical?
- What would count as support?
- What would count as disconfirmation?
- Are alternative explanations considered?
- Is clinical usefulness being confused with theoretical proof?

## Culture and history

- What cultural, historical, religious, linguistic, and political contexts shape the material?
- Is a symbol being treated as universal too quickly?
- Are local meanings preserved?
- Are non-Western, Indigenous, or marginalized traditions being used as evidence for a theory not their own?

## Gender and power

- Does the concept encode gender essentialism?
- Does it reproduce racialized, colonial, or civilizational assumptions?
- Does it treat European modernity as the developmental norm?
- Does it acknowledge social and institutional dimensions of the unconscious?

## Revision

- Can the concept be reformulated more modestly?
- What remains valuable after critique?
- What should be abandoned?
- What should be treated as metaphor rather than mechanism?
