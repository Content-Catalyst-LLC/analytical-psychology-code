# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about real-world psychology, therapy outcomes, Jungian theory, clinical process, culture, gender, race, or religion.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each Jungian concept family precisely.
2. Separate interpretive usefulness from empirical validity.
3. Distinguish clinical, hermeneutic, phenomenological, philosophical, and empirical claims.
4. Review psychotherapy outcome evidence where relevant.
5. Use validated measures where appropriate.
6. Obtain ethical approval for human-subjects research.
7. Include feminist, queer, postcolonial, racial, cultural, and religious-studies scholarship.
8. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
9. Avoid using symbolic resonance as proof.
10. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian theory. It does not prove or disprove archetypes, the collective unconscious, individuation, anima/animus, shadow, persona, or synchronicity. It does not rank cultures or evaluate religions.
