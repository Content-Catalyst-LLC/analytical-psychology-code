# Data Dictionary

The files in `data/raw/` are synthetic and illustrative. They do not represent real patients, spiritual experiences, religious communities, clinical cases, crisis outcomes, or empirical findings.

## `synthetic_numinous_crisis.csv`

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Synthetic row identifier |
| `symbolic_environment` | Illustrative setting or support condition |
| `time` | Synthetic time period |
| `numinous_intensity` | Stylized sacred or archetypal intensity |
| `symbolic_containment` | Stylized capacity to hold symbolic material without literalization, inflation, or collapse |
| `ego_stability` | Stylized ordinary functioning, reality-testing, and self-continuity |
| `trauma_vulnerability` | Stylized vulnerability to trauma reactivation, dissociation, or affective flooding |
| `shadow_awareness` | Stylized humility, self-knowledge, and capacity to recognize projection |
| `relational_support` | Stylized availability of grounded witnesses, community, clinicians, elders, or trusted others |
| `ritual_containment` | Stylized strength of ritual, tradition, liturgy, symbolic law, or communal practice |
| `sleep_disruption` | Stylized sleep loss and rhythm disruption |
| `practice_intensity` | Stylized intensity of meditation, retreat, ritual, or spiritual practice |
| `perceived_mission` | Stylized special calling, chosenness, or apocalyptic urgency |
| `humility_limit_awareness` | Stylized awareness of ordinary limits, responsibility, and human scale |
