# Numinous Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist when thinking conceptually about numinous experience, spiritual emergency, and symbolic crisis.

## Safety and functioning

- Is the person sleeping, eating, and able to meet ordinary responsibilities?
- Is there loss of reality-testing, severe dissociation, mania, psychosis, command voices, or danger to self or others?
- Is immediate clinical, emergency, or crisis support needed before interpretation?
- Has spiritual practice intensity exceeded the person’s capacity to regulate?

## Symbolic relation

- Can the person hold the experience symbolically rather than literally?
- Can they tolerate uncertainty and receive feedback?
- Does the experience increase humility or inflate specialness?
- Does the experience widen life or narrow it around private certainty?

## Shadow and humility

- Is shadow being recognized or projected onto critics, family, outsiders, or ordinary limits?
- Is perceived mission balanced by humility and responsibility?
- Does the person remain accountable to ordinary ethics?
- Is the experience producing service, compassion, and truthfulness or entitlement and contempt?

## Containment

- What ritual, communal, clinical, relational, or embodied container is available?
- Is the person isolated with overwhelming symbolic material?
- Is the community stabilizing or intensifying the crisis?
- Does the person need rest, grounding, reduced practice intensity, or practical support?

## Cultural and religious humility

- What religious or cultural tradition does the experience belong to?
- Who has legitimate interpretive authority in that tradition?
- Could psychological language erase tradition-specific meaning?
- Could spiritual language obscure trauma, dissociation, or clinical risk?
