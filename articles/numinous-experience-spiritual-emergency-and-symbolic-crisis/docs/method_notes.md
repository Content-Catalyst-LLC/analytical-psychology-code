# Method Notes

This scaffold uses synthetic conceptual variables to illustrate the article’s argument. The variables are not validated psychological, clinical, or spiritual scales and should not be interpreted as measurements of real people, communities, traditions, religious experiences, or clinical outcomes.

## Modeling logic

The article’s core claim is that numinous experience becomes dangerous when sacred intensity outruns containment, ego stability, shadow awareness, and relational support.

A simplified crisis-risk model is:

```text
crisis risk =
  numinous intensity
  + trauma vulnerability
  + practice intensity
  + sleep disruption
  - symbolic containment
  - ego stability
  - shadow awareness
  - relational support
```

A simplified inflation-risk model is:

```text
inflation risk =
  numinous intensity
  + perceived mission
  - shadow awareness
  - humility / limitation awareness
  - relational support
```

A simplified integration-potential model is:

```text
integration potential =
  symbolic containment
  + ego stability
  + shadow awareness
  + relational support
  + humility / limitation awareness
  - crisis risk
  - inflation risk
```

## Limits

These examples are conceptual demonstrations. They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- spiritual direction;
- crisis intervention;
- treatment assignment;
- employee screening;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual scoring;
- clinical triage;
- claims about empirical effectiveness;
- proof or disproof of religious, spiritual, Jungian, or transpersonal claims.

## Recommended use

Use these files to demonstrate reproducible research structure, transparent assumptions, synthetic simulation, dynamic symbol-network modeling, and conceptual-method documentation in scholarly writing.
