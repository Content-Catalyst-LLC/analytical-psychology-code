# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- private dream interpretation;
- religious reductionism;
- cultural extraction;
- interpretation of sacred material outside appropriate context;
- claims of cultural hierarchy;
- claims of gender essence;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about motif recurrence, symbolic clusters, latent patterning, semantic networks, and alternative explanations, but it cannot replace close reading, clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, cultural context, religious literacy, or historical analysis.
