# Collective Unconscious Interpretation Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, dream dictionary, religious interpretation engine, or proof system for Jungian theory.

## Description before interpretation

- What image appears?
- What happens in sequence?
- What affect is present?
- What source context is involved: dream, myth, religious text, literature, ritual, or cultural memory?
- What remains ambiguous?

## Layer distinction

- What belongs to personal biography?
- What belongs to the immediate text, dream, ritual, or narrative?
- What belongs to cultural, religious, or historical context?
- What belongs to genre convention or transmission?
- What may plausibly be archetypal or transpersonal?

## Rival explanations

- Is recurrence due to direct borrowing?
- Is recurrence due to shared genre convention?
- Is recurrence due to religious education or textual transmission?
- Is recurrence due to shared developmental pressure?
- Is recurrence due to embodied cognition?
- Is recurrence due to historical or intergenerational memory?

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce living traditions to psychological examples.
- Do not treat similarity as sameness.
- Do not turn ordinary conflict into cosmic drama.
- Do not impose gender essence.
- Do not interpret private dreams without context and consent.
- Do not use archetypal language to excuse harm.
- Do not confuse symbolic recurrence with proof of universal psychic inheritance.

## Responsible conclusion

- Keep claims proportional to evidence.
- Name alternatives.
- Preserve cultural specificity.
- Distinguish motif, image, symbol, complex, archetype, and collective-unconscious hypothesis.
- Treat collective-unconscious interpretation as hypothesis, not certainty.
