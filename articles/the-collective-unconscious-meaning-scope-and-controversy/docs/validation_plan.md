# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or scholarly extension would require careful validation before making any claim about the collective unconscious, archetypal recurrence, symbolic universality, dream interpretation, religious symbolism, cultural transmission, historical memory, or psychological structure.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define motifs and symbolic clusters before analysis.
2. Separate collective-unconscious hypotheses from cultural transmission, genre convention, developmental conditions, embodied cognition, ecological context, ritual function, theology, and historical borrowing.
3. Use transparent corpus-selection criteria.
4. Avoid treating similarity as proof of sameness.
5. Include counterexamples and non-recurring motifs.
6. Compare motif recurrence across controlled corpora.
7. Test whether recurrence persists after accounting for genre, diffusion, translation, and shared source traditions.
8. Work with historians, religious studies scholars, anthropologists, linguists, psychologists, clinicians, and cultural experts when analyzing real traditions.
9. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, private dreams, clinical material, or sacred community material without consent and ethical approval.
10. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
11. Pair computational findings with close reading, contextual interpretation, ethical caution, and cultural humility.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not diagnose people, interpret private dreams, prove Jungian theory, reduce religion to psychology, rank cultures, or replace scholarly interpretation. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
