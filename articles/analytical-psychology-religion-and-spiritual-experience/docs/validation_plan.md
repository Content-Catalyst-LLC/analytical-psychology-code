# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical, clinical, religious-studies, theological, or transpersonal-psychology extension would require careful validation before making any claim about real religious experience, spiritual development, symbolic vitality, clinical outcomes, or religious traditions.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate religious interpretation, theological claims, clinical assessment, and Jungian symbolic amplification.
3. Use validated clinical or psychological measures where appropriate.
4. Work with qualified clinicians for mental-health or spiritual-crisis research.
5. Work with tradition-informed scholars, theologians, chaplains, or spiritual-care experts where religious contexts are involved.
6. Obtain ethical approval for human-subjects research.
7. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that purpose.
8. Include disconfirming cases and alternative interpretations.
9. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
10. Use case material only with consent, anonymization, and ethical safeguards.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian theory, diagnose spiritual states, evaluate religious truth, recommend treatment, provide spiritual direction, or rank religious traditions. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
