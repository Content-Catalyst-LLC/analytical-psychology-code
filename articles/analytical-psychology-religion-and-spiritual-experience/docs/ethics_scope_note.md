# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- spiritual direction;
- religious authority;
- crisis intervention;
- treatment recommendation;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about religion, spiritual experience, symbolic containment, numinosity, ritual support, shadow awareness, inflation, religious trauma pressure, and integration potential, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, spiritual-care discernment, theological reasoning, empirical validation, or therapeutic containment.
