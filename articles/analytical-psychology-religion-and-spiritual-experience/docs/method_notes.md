## Limits

These examples are conceptual demonstrations. They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- spiritual direction;
- religious authority;
- treatment assignment;
- employee screening;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual scoring;
- clinical triage;
- claims about empirical effectiveness;
- proof or disproof of religious, spiritual, Jungian, or theological claims.
