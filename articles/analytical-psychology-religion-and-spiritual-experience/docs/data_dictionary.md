# Data Dictionary

The files in `data/raw/` are synthetic and illustrative. They do not represent real patients, religious traditions, spiritual experiences, religious communities, clinical cases, or empirical findings.

## `synthetic_religion_spiritual_experience.csv`

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Synthetic row identifier |
| `religious_environment` | Illustrative symbolic, religious, or spiritual environment |
| `time` | Synthetic time period |
| `numinous_intensity` | Stylized sacred or archetypal intensity |
| `symbolic_containment` | Stylized capacity to hold religious or spiritual material without literalism, inflation, or collapse |
| `ego_stability` | Stylized ordinary functioning, reflective distance, and self-continuity |
| `shadow_awareness` | Stylized humility and capacity to recognize projection, aggression, guilt, dependency, and power |
| `ritual_support` | Stylized strength of ritual, liturgy, practice, communal rhythm, or embodied symbolic form |
| `humility_limit_awareness` | Stylized awareness of ordinary limits, fallibility, and human scale |
| `perceived_mission` | Stylized special calling, chosenness, or private authority |
| `doctrinal_rigidity` | Stylized literalism, defensive certainty, or symbolic closure |
| `symbolic_vitality` | Stylized living symbolic force and capacity to mediate meaning |
| `religious_trauma_pressure` | Stylized pressure from shame, coercion, spiritual abuse, persecutory God-image, or symbolic injury |
| `communal_memory` | Stylized strength of inherited religious story, collective memory, and tradition |
