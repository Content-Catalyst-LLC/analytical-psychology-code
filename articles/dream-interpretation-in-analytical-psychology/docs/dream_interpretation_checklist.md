# Dream Interpretation Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, crisis tool, or dream-interpretation service.

## Context

- What is the dreamer’s conscious attitude?
- What life situation is active?
- What affect surrounds the dream?
- What does the dreamer associate with each major image?

## Structure

- Where does the dream begin?
- What interrupts the opening situation?
- What movement occurs: inward, outward, downward, upward, across, or around?
- Where does the emotional intensity shift?
- What remains unresolved?

## Compensation

- What has waking life excluded, exaggerated, denied, or neglected?
- Does the dream present contrary or missing material?
- Is the compensation local, bodily, relational, typological, moral, or archetypal?

## Amplification

- Does the dream image need mythic, religious, literary, ritual, or cultural amplification?
- Is amplification grounded in the dreamer’s actual context?
- Is the interpreter preserving cultural and historical difference?

## Series

- Does the image recur?
- Does it change over time?
- Does a figure become more or less threatening?
- Does a locked room open, a bridge repair, or a center emerge?

## Ethical caution

- Is the interpretation becoming too certain?
- Is the dream being literalized as external fact?
- Is symbolic interpretation being used where clinical safety, grounding, or professional care is needed?
- Is the dreamer’s own association being subordinated to theory?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not treat dream motifs as fixed codes.
- Do not interpret real dreams automatically.
- Do not impose archetypal meaning where local context is enough.
- Do not romanticize trauma or crisis.
- Keep interpretation provisional, contextual, humble, and ethically grounded.
