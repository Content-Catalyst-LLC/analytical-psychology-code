# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about dreams, compensation, symbolic recurrence, motif centrality, development, therapy, diagnosis, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, qualitative research, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat dream-network outputs as interpretations of real dreams.
4. Use synthetic, public-domain, or ethically approved materials only.
5. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, statisticians, dream researchers, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable dream journals, therapy notes, clinical records, or private life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
8. Compare Jungian interpretation with alternative explanations, including trauma repetition, memory consolidation, threat simulation, affect regulation, narrative identity, and predictive processing.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Pair computational findings with close reading and clinical/contextual judgment.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian dream interpretation, diagnose people, interpret real dreams, recommend treatment, predict the future, replace clinical judgment, or prove analytical psychology. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
