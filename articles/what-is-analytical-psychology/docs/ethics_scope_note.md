# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- mental-health evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- crisis intervention;
- clinical decision-making;
- private dream interpretation;
- religious reductionism;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- reputation scoring;
- social scoring;
- personality assessment;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling;
- claims of cultural hierarchy;
- uncritical repetition of gender essentialism.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about symbolic recurrence, motif clustering, co-occurrence networks, compensation, complexes, and analytical-psychology concepts, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, personal context, social analysis, religious literacy, cultural history, or accountability.
