# Symbolic Analysis Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, dream dictionary, personality test, or automated interpretation system.

## Before interpretation

- What is the source type: dream, myth, literature, clinical narrative, active imagination, religious text, or cultural image?
- Who produced the material, and under what conditions?
- What is the language, genre, tradition, and historical context?
- What personal associations are available?
- What affective tone accompanies the image?
- What sequence does the image belong to?

## During interpretation

- Is the interpretation grounded in the material itself?
- Is the symbol being treated as fixed code or living image?
- Is cultural context being preserved?
- Are social and material realities being acknowledged?
- Are alternative explanations considered?
- Is amplification being used after personal/contextual interpretation rather than before it?

## After interpretation

- Are claims proportional to evidence?
- Is uncertainty stated clearly?
- Are clinical, religious, and cultural boundaries respected?
- Is the interpretation ethically useful or merely dramatic?
- Does the analysis avoid gender essentialism, cultural flattening, and symbolic imperialism?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not automate private symbolic interpretation.
- Do not reduce religion or culture to psychology.
- Do not treat co-occurrence as proof.
- Keep symbolic interpretation provisional, contextual, and accountable.
