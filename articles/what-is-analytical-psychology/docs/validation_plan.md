# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious scholarly or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about symbolism, dreams, complexes, archetypes, collective patterning, trauma, development, or therapeutic change.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define symbolic terms and concepts before coding.
2. Separate personal association, cultural context, textual evidence, and archetypal amplification.
3. Use consented, ethically approved, or public-domain corpora only.
4. Avoid private dream interpretation without informed consent and proper clinical context.
5. Compare computational patterns with close reading.
6. Preserve language, genre, culture, ritual context, and historical specificity.
7. Include competing explanations from cognitive psychology, narrative psychology, anthropology, religious studies, trauma studies, and literary criticism.
8. Treat model outputs as prompts for interpretation, not as conclusions.
9. Include clear uncertainty and limitations.
10. Do not use symbolic analysis for employment, diagnosis, surveillance, individual assessment, or automated psychological judgment.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not prove analytical psychology, validate archetypes, diagnose individuals, interpret private dreams, reduce religion to psychology, replace therapy, or settle debates in Jungian studies. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
