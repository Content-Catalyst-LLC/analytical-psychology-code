# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- mental-health evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- crisis intervention;
- clinical decision-making;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- reputation scoring;
- social scoring;
- personality assessment;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about affective recurrence, complex activation, trigger response, regulation, symbolic repetition, projection, and transference, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, personal context, social analysis, or accountability.
