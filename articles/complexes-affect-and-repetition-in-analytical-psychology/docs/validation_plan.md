# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about complex activation, affective recurrence, projection, transference, trauma, regulation, relational buffering, mental health, or therapeutic change.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, cognitive psychology, trauma studies, attachment theory, affective neuroscience, psychodynamic theory, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat model outputs as measurements of real people.
4. Use synthetic, public-domain, or ethically approved materials only.
5. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, trauma specialists, statisticians, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, clinical records, employment records, HR files, social-media records, relationship records, or private life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
8. Compare Jungian complex theory with alternative explanations, including schema theory, attachment theory, trauma theory, object relations, cognitive appraisal, institutional conditions, and social context.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Pair computational findings with clinical/contextual judgment, ethical accountability, and social analysis where relevant.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not diagnose people, assess personality, evaluate employees, recommend treatment, predict mental-health outcomes, replace therapy, or prove analytical psychology. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
