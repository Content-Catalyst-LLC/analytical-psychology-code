# Complex Recurrence Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, employment assessment, personality test, or prediction system.

## Recognition

- What affect returns with unusual force?
- What situation repeatedly activates it?
- Does the reaction exceed the present situation?
- What bodily state appears first?
- What relational role keeps returning?

## Structure

- What memories, fantasies, expectations, and defenses cluster around the affect?
- What symbolic images recur in dreams or imagination?
- What trigger pattern is most reliable?
- What response pattern follows activation?

## Context

- Is the repeated pattern intrapsychic, relational, social, institutional, or structural?
- Is the affect proportionate to real threat or injustice?
- Is trauma stabilization needed before interpretation?
- Who benefits or suffers if the repetition is interpreted as a complex?

## Transformation

- Is there capacity for reflective pause?
- Is there relational buffering or trusted witness?
- Can the person distinguish feeling from total interpretation?
- Can the repeated scene be symbolized rather than enacted?
- What new response becomes possible?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not pathologize appropriate affect.
- Do not reduce structural harm to personal complex.
- Do not romanticize repetition.
- Keep interpretation provisional, contextual, trauma-informed, and ethically grounded.
