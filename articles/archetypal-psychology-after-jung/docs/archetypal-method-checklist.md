# Archetypal Method Checklist

Use this checklist when applying archetypal or Hillman-influenced interpretation.

## Image attention

- What is the exact image, mood, figure, or scene?
- What color, tempo, atmosphere, texture, and posture does it have?
- What world does the image create?
- What is lost if the image is immediately translated into a concept?
- What metaphors does the image itself invite?

## Polycentric reading

- Which imaginal centers or psychic styles are active?
- Are multiple gods, moods, or figures making competing claims?
- Is integration being imposed too quickly?
- What does each imaginal figure see or value?
- Where is conflict a plurality of styles rather than a simple pathology?

## Clinical boundary

- Is the person stable enough for imaginal deepening?
- Is there acute risk, trauma overwhelm, dissociation, psychosis, or suicidality?
- What containment, grounding, relational support, or practical care is needed?
- Is poetic reading replacing necessary clinical action?
- Is social or material suffering being aestheticized?

## Cultural reading

- Does archetypal interpretation preserve historical and political specificity?
- Are material conditions being dissolved into free-floating symbolism?
- What does the image do culturally, institutionally, and politically?
- What other methods are needed alongside archetypal interpretation?
