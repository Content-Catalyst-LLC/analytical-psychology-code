# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about real psychotherapy, clinical outcomes, dream interpretation, archetypal psychology, imaginal work, or symptom transformation.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate metaphorical interpretation from empirical claim.
3. Distinguish literary, cultural, clinical, phenomenological, and philosophical uses.
4. Use validated measures where appropriate.
5. Obtain ethical approval for human-subjects research.
6. Avoid individual-level scoring unless the instrument is validated for that purpose.
7. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
8. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
9. Include clinical containment and risk variables.
10. Use case material only with proper consent, anonymization, and ethical safeguards.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate archetypal psychology, prove archetypes, diagnose imaginal states, evaluate dreams, score symptoms, or rank psychological approaches. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
