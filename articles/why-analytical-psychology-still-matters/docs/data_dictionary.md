# Data Dictionary

The dataset in `data/raw/synthetic_symbolic_relevance.csv` is synthetic and illustrative. It does not represent real people, patients, clinicians, institutions, or research participants.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Synthetic row identifier |
| `tradition_type` | Illustrative type of psychological model or interpretive tradition |
| `time` | Synthetic time period |
| `symbolic_depth` | Stylized measure of attention to symbol, dream, myth, image, and unconscious meaning |
| `meaning_coherence` | Stylized measure of a framework’s ability to organize existential and narrative meaning |
| `clinical_utility` | Stylized measure of practical therapeutic usefulness |
| `cultural_interpretive_power` | Stylized measure of usefulness for interpreting cultural imagery, myth, projection, and collective fantasy |
| `revision_capacity` | Stylized measure of openness to critique, evidence, historical context, trauma research, and contemporary revision |
| `doctrinal_rigidity` | Stylized measure of closed-system thinking or resistance to revision |
| `symbolic_loss` | Stylized pressure representing weakening symbolic containers in modern life |
| `projection_intensity` | Stylized pressure representing heightened displacement of disowned material onto others |
| `existential_dislocation` | Stylized pressure representing loss of meaning, orientation, and psychic coherence |
| `institutional_mistrust` | Stylized pressure representing weakened trust in shared institutions and interpretive authorities |
