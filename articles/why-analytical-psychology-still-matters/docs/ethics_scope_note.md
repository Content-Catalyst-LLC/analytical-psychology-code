# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Analytical psychology works with symbol, dream, projection, meaning, and unconscious life. These concepts require care, humility, and interpretive restraint. Computational scaffolds can help clarify assumptions, but they cannot replace clinical judgment, lived context, ethical interpretation, or empirical validation.
