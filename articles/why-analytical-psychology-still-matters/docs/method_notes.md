# Method Notes

This scaffold uses synthetic conceptual variables to illustrate the article’s argument. The variables are not validated psychological scales and should not be interpreted as measurements of individuals.

## Modeling logic

The article’s core claim is that analytical psychology remains relevant when symbolic depth, meaning coherence, clinical utility, cultural interpretation, and revision capacity are held together. The synthetic workflows represent that argument as:

```text
contemporary relevance =
  symbolic depth
  + meaning coherence
  + clinical utility
  + cultural interpretive power
  + revision capacity
  - doctrinal rigidity
```

The model also represents modern demand for depth-psychological meaning as a function of symbolic loss, projection intensity, existential dislocation, and institutional mistrust.

## Limits

These examples are conceptual demonstrations. They should not be used for:

- clinical diagnosis;
- psychological assessment;
- employee screening;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual scoring;
- therapy triage;
- claims about empirical effectiveness.

## Recommended use

Use these files to demonstrate reproducible research structure, transparent assumptions, synthetic simulation, and conceptual modeling in scholarly writing.
