# Data Dictionary

The files in `data/raw/` are synthetic and illustrative. They do not represent real children, families, clinical records, school records, developmental assessments, treatment outcomes, or empirical findings.

## `synthetic_childhood_development.csv`

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Synthetic row identifier |
| `developmental_context` | Illustrative developmental field |
| `time` | Synthetic developmental time marker |
| `ego_differentiation` | Stylized emergence of ego capacity and self/other differentiation |
| `relational_security` | Stylized secure base, reliability, and capacity for exploration |
| `caregiver_mirroring` | Stylized recognition, attunement, and emotional mirroring |
| `bodily_regulation` | Stylized embodied regulation and tolerance of affect |
| `symbolic_play` | Stylized flexibility of play, imagination, image-making, and symbolic transformation |
| `affect_regulation` | Stylized ability to feel without flooding, collapse, or dissociation |
| `complex_activation` | Stylized activation of affectively charged relational patterns |
| `family_tension` | Stylized household strain, unspoken anxiety, conflict, or emotional projection |
| `symbolic_capacity` | Stylized capacity for dream, fantasy, image, story, and metaphor |
| `developmental_coherence` | Stylized coordination of ego, relation, body, affect, and symbol |
| `future_personality` | Stylized later personality organization shaped by developmental relations |
