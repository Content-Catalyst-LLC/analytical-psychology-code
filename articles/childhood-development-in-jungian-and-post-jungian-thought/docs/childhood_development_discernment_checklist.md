# Childhood Development Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a treatment protocol, child assessment, or parenting evaluation.

## Developmental field

- What relational field is shaping the child’s development?
- Is the child experiencing enough reliability, attunement, and repair?
- Is the child being recognized as an individual rather than as a carrier of parental projection?
- Are family myths, cultural expectations, and institutional pressures being considered?

## Ego and symbolic development

- Is ego differentiation being supported without crushing imagination?
- Is symbolic play flexible, relational, and capable of transformation?
- Are drawings, stories, dreams, or play sequences being interpreted carefully rather than mechanically?
- Is the child’s own affective response guiding interpretation?

## Complex formation

- What repeating emotional pattern may be organizing the child’s experience?
- Is a parental, sibling, family, shame, abandonment, achievement, or fear complex forming?
- Is the child carrying unspoken family tension or parental unconsciousness?
- Is the complex being treated as a pattern, not a fixed destiny?

## Trauma and disruption

- Is the child safe?
- Is play symbolic and flexible, or rigid and repetitive?
- Are dissociation, bodily symptoms, nightmares, aggression, withdrawal, or premature caretaking present?
- Is concrete protection being prioritized before symbolic interpretation?

## Ethical restraint

- Is the child being over-symbolized?
- Are social conditions such as poverty, racism, disability, war, migration, or institutional harm being considered?
- Is Jungian language being used to deepen care rather than replace developmental expertise?
- Does the interpretation serve the child’s process, not the adult’s desire for depth?
