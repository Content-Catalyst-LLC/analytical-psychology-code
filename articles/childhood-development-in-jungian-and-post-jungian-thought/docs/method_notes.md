# Method Notes

This scaffold uses synthetic conceptual variables to illustrate the article’s argument. The variables are not validated developmental, clinical, educational, diagnostic, therapeutic, or child-assessment measures and should not be interpreted as measurements of real children, families, schools, clinicians, or developmental outcomes.

## Modeling logic

The article’s core claim is that childhood development in Jungian and post-Jungian thought is the early formation of psyche through ego differentiation, relational security, caregiver mirroring, bodily regulation, symbolic play, affect regulation, complex formation, family atmosphere, culture, and future personality organization.

A simplified developmental-coherence model is:

```text
developmental coherence =
  ego differentiation
  + relational security
  + symbolic capacity
  + affect regulation
  + play flexibility
  - complex activation
  - developmental strain
```

A simplified developmental-strain model is:

```text
developmental strain =
  (ego differentiation - relational security)^2
  + (symbolic capacity - relational security)^2
  + complex activation^2
```

A simplified symbolic-capacity model is:

```text
symbolic capacity =
  symbolic play
  + caregiver mirroring
  + bodily regulation
  + relational security
  - family tension
```

## Limits

These examples are conceptual demonstrations. They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- child assessment;
- educational evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- clinical decision-making;
- school placement;
- employment screening;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual scoring;
- individual evaluation;
- claims about empirical effectiveness;
- proof or disproof of Jungian developmental theory.

## Recommended use

Use these files to demonstrate reproducible research structure, transparent assumptions, synthetic simulation, dynamic child-development network modeling, and conceptual-method documentation in scholarly writing.
