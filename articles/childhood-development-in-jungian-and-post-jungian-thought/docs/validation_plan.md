# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical, educational, or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about child development, symbolic play, ego formation, attachment, trauma, educational outcomes, clinical outcomes, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, research measurement, and conceptual modeling.
3. Use validated developmental, attachment, symptom, play, family, and affect-regulation measures where appropriate.
4. Work with qualified developmental psychologists, clinicians, educators, caregivers, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
5. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that purpose.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable child, family, school, or clinical material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include cultural, linguistic, disability, socioeconomic, institutional, and family context.
8. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
9. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
10. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not developmental truth.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian child-development theory, diagnose children, recommend treatment, evaluate school performance, assess parenting, predict development, or replace clinical/developmental judgment. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
