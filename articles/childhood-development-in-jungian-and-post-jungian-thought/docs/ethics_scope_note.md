# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- child assessment;
- educational evaluation;
- clinical decision-making;
- treatment recommendation;
- parenting evaluation;
- school placement;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about childhood development, ego differentiation, relational security, caregiver mirroring, bodily regulation, symbolic play, affect regulation, complex activation, family atmosphere, and future personality, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, developmental expertise, ethical care, empirical validation, cultural context, or child protection.
