# Childhood Development in Jungian and Post-Jungian Thought

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Childhood Development in Jungian and Post-Jungian Thought”** in the Analytical Psychology, Symbolism & the Depth Mind knowledge series.

The repository materials translate the article’s argument into reproducible, synthetic-data workflows. The goal is not to diagnose children, evaluate development, assess educational performance, recommend treatment, or reduce childhood to a formula. The goal is to demonstrate how ego differentiation, relational security, caregiver mirroring, bodily regulation, symbolic play, affect regulation, complex activation, family tension, developmental coherence, and future personality can be represented transparently in conceptual modeling.

## Repository purpose

This article argues that childhood development in Jungian and post-Jungian thought is not merely the accumulation of skills or the progressive emergence of rational control. It is the early formation of psyche through relationship, affect, fantasy, symbol, body, play, family atmosphere, complexes, attachment, and the gradual differentiation of consciousness.

The code examples extend that argument through:

- synthetic childhood-development data;
- developmental-coherence and symbolic-capacity simulation;
- complex-formation and family-atmosphere modeling;
- caregiver-mirroring and play-flexibility workflows;
- dynamic child-development network scripts;
- reproducible tables and figures;
- multi-language computational scaffolding;
- documented responsible-use limits.

## Responsible-use statement

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, conceptual modeling, symbolic-process analysis, institutional learning, and reproducible workflows. They are not intended for diagnosis, therapy, psychological assessment, educational evaluation, child assessment, clinical decision-making, treatment recommendation, employment screening, workplace surveillance, individual performance management, or individual evaluation.

## Suggested workflow

1. Review `article-metadata.yml` for article context.
2. Inspect the synthetic data in `data/raw/synthetic_childhood_development.csv`.
3. Run the R workflow in `r/simulate_childhood_development.R`.
4. Run the Python workflow in `python/childhood_development_network.py`.
5. Use `sql/schema.sql` to inspect the childhood-development schema.
6. Treat all outputs as illustrative and synthetic, not empirical findings, assessment scores, or clinical claims.
