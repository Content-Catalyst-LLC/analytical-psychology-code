# Dream Series Interpretation Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, crisis tool, or dream-interpretation service.

## Compensation

- What conscious attitude might the dream be answering?
- What has waking life excluded, exaggerated, denied, or neglected?
- Does the dream present contrary material?
- Is the compensation local, personal, typological, moral, bodily, relational, or archetypal?

## Prospection

- Does the dream contain symbols of emergence, transition, development, orientation, or integration?
- Do these symbols appear across a series, or only once?
- Is there evidence of symbolic development over time?
- Could the image be retrospective, defensive, traumatic, or wishful rather than prospective?

## Series pattern

- Are recurring figures changing?
- Are spaces becoming more or less accessible?
- Are roads, bridges, rooms, or centers becoming more coherent?
- Are compensatory images being integrated or repeated unchanged?

## Clinical and ethical caution

- Is the dreamer in acute distress, crisis, psychosis, mania, severe dissociation, or unstable reality testing?
- Is symbolic interpretation being used to avoid practical care?
- Is a prospective image being literalized as destiny or command?
- Is the interpretation preserving humility and uncertainty?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not treat prospective dreams as prophecy.
- Do not use models to interpret real dreams automatically.
- Do not impose a developmental story on ambiguous material.
- Do not romanticize suffering or repetition.
- Pair symbolic analysis with context, life reality, and ethical care.
