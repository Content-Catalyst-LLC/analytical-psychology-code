# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about real psychotherapy, clinical outcomes, dream interpretation, psychodynamic process, or Jungian/Freudian explanatory adequacy.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate clinical interpretation from empirical claim.
3. Use validated measures where appropriate.
4. Review psychoanalytic, psychodynamic, and Jungian outcome research.
5. Obtain ethical approval for human-subjects research.
6. Avoid individual-level scoring unless the instrument is validated for that purpose.
7. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
8. Distinguish illustrative modeling from evidence.
9. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
10. Use case material only with proper consent, anonymization, and ethical safeguards.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Freud, Jung, psychoanalysis, analytical psychology, dream interpretation, archetypal theory, libido theory, or the collective unconscious. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
