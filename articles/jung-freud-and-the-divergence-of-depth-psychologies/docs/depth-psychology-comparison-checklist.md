# Depth Psychology Comparison Checklist

Use this checklist when comparing Freudian and Jungian interpretations.

## Freudian questions

- What wish, fear, conflict, or defense may be disguised?
- What childhood or developmental history is active?
- What is being repeated in transference?
- What is being resisted?
- What is the symptom doing psychically?

## Jungian questions

- What does the image itself present?
- How might the dream compensate conscious one-sidedness?
- What symbolic or mythic field does the image evoke?
- What future-oriented or developmental possibility is implied?
- How do shadow, persona, Self, or individuation appear?

## Integrative questions

- Is this material better approached through conflict, symbol, or both?
- Is interpretation becoming reductive?
- Is amplification becoming inflated?
- What would make the interpretation more clinically grounded?
- What would make the interpretation more symbolically adequate?
