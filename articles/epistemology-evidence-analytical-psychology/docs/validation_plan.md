# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical extension would require careful validation before making any claims about real-world psychology, therapy outcomes, symbolic interpretation, archetypes, or clinical process.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define claim types explicitly.
2. Separate empirical hypotheses from clinical heuristics, hermeneutic models, phenomenological descriptions, philosophical metaphors, and speculative claims.
3. Review existing psychotherapy outcome evidence.
4. Use validated measures where appropriate.
5. Obtain ethical approval for human-subjects research.
6. Avoid individual-level scoring unless the instrument is validated for that purpose.
7. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
8. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
9. Treat cultural and religious material with historical and linguistic specificity.
10. Distinguish interpretive usefulness from theoretical truth.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian theory. It does not prove archetypes, prove the collective unconscious, measure dream validity, diagnose symbolic material, or assess clinical outcomes.
