# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- mental-health evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- clinical decision-making;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling;
- claims of cultural hierarchy;
- reduction of religious traditions to psychological examples;
- uncritical repetition of gender essentialism.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about Jungian concept formation, network centrality, phase activation, and post-Jungian revision, but it cannot replace historical scholarship, clinical judgment, ethical care, textual interpretation, cultural context, or accountability.
