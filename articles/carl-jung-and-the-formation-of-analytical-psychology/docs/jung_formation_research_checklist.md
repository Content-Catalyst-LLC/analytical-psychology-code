# Jung Formation Research Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a proof system for Jungian theory.

## Historical grounding

- Which period of Jung’s work is being discussed?
- Is the claim based on a primary text, correspondence, lecture, biography, or later interpretation?
- Is the concept being used in Jung’s original sense, a later Jungian sense, or a contemporary revision?
- Is the historical context clear enough?

## Conceptual distinction

- Is complex theory being distinguished from archetype theory?
- Is the personal unconscious being distinguished from the collective unconscious?
- Is symbol being distinguished from sign, metaphor, image, and doctrine?
- Is individuation being distinguished from simple self-expression or personal branding?
- Is post-Jungian revision being distinguished from Jung’s original formulation?

## Critical discipline

- Does the interpretation avoid gender essentialism?
- Does the interpretation preserve cultural and religious specificity?
- Does the interpretation avoid treating resemblance as sameness?
- Does the interpretation avoid turning network centrality into truth?
- Does the interpretation include limits, alternatives, and historical uncertainty?

## Responsible conclusion

- Keep claims proportional to evidence.
- Name the period and concept clearly.
- Treat Jung as historically important but not beyond revision.
- Treat analytical psychology as a living tradition of inheritance, critique, and reconstruction.
