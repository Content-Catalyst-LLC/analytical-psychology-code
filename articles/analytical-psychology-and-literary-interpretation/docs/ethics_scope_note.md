# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for literary-method demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- author diagnosis;
- psychological assessment;
- student grading;
- automated literary interpretation;
- cultural ranking;
- literary canon ranking;
- employment selection;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual evaluation.

Literary-symbolic modeling can help identify patterns for close reading, but it cannot replace formal analysis, historical interpretation, cultural specificity, reader-response complexity, or ethical judgment.
