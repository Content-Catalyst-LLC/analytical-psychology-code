# Jungian Literary Method Checklist

Use this checklist when applying analytical psychology to literary interpretation.

## Textual grounding

- What specific image, word, motif, scene, or formal pattern supports the interpretation?
- Is the motif repeated, structurally central, or emotionally charged?
- How do syntax, voice, genre, irony, and narrative structure shape the symbol?
- Does the interpretation arise from the text, or is it imposed from a template?

## Symbolic interpretation

- What shadow, double, threshold, wound, guide, child, house, or descent pattern appears?
- Is the image personal, collective, historical, religious, political, or several at once?
- Does the symbol transform across the work?
- Does the text stage integration, failed integration, inflation, projection, fragmentation, or refusal?

## Historical and cultural specificity

- What cultural, linguistic, religious, racial, gendered, class, colonial, or political context shapes the image?
- Is archetypal language flattening cultural difference?
- Does the reading preserve the symbol’s tradition-specific meaning?
- Does the interpretation account for power and historical violence?

## Methodological restraint

- Are Jungian concepts used as questions rather than labels?
- Have alternative readings been considered?
- Does the reading honor literary form before theory?
- Is the conclusion modest enough for the evidence?
