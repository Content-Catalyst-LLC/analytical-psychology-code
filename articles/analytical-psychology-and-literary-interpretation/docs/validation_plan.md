# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious literary-digital-humanities extension would require a much more careful validation and interpretive workflow.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define the corpus selection criteria.
2. Document edition choices, translation status, and copyright constraints.
3. Document motif dictionary construction and revision.
4. Separate literal uses of motif terms from symbolic uses.
5. Use close reading to validate motif relevance.
6. Compare automated motif detection against human annotation.
7. Include historical, linguistic, genre, and cultural metadata.
8. Distinguish archetypal recurrence from literary convention, allusion, and genre inheritance.
9. Include disconfirming cases and ambiguous passages.
10. Avoid claims about authors’ psychology unless supported by appropriate evidence and framed cautiously.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not automate interpretation, prove archetypes, diagnose authors, rank texts, evaluate students, or replace literary criticism. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
