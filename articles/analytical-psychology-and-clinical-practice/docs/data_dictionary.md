# Data Dictionary

The files in `data/raw/` are synthetic and illustrative. They do not represent real patients, therapists, treatment outcomes, clinical notes, supervision records, or empirical findings.

## `synthetic_clinical_practice.csv`

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Synthetic row identifier |
| `clinical_presentation` | Illustrative clinical presentation category |
| `session` | Synthetic session marker |
| `symptom_burden` | Stylized distress, impairment, or symptom pressure |
| `ego_integration` | Stylized reflective continuity, reality testing, and capacity to relate to inner life |
| `symbolic_capacity` | Stylized capacity to dream, imagine, symbolize, and reflect |
| `relational_safety` | Stylized therapeutic alliance, containment, boundaries, and treatment-field safety |
| `affect_regulation` | Stylized ability to feel and regulate affect |
| `complex_activation` | Stylized activation of affective-memory clusters and repetitive patterns |
| `conscious_onesidedness` | Stylized rigidity of conscious attitude or persona overidentification |
| `trauma_fragmentation` | Stylized dissociation, trauma fragmentation, and discontinuity |
| `shame_load` | Stylized developmental shame pressure |
| `dream_richness` | Stylized vitality and complexity of dream material |
| `shadow_awareness` | Stylized capacity to recognize and integrate disowned psychic material |
