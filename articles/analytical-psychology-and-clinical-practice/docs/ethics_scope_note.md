# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- clinical decision-making;
- treatment recommendation;
- therapist evaluation;
- supervision judgment;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about analytical psychology in clinical practice, symptom burden, ego integration, symbolic capacity, relational safety, affect regulation, complexes, compensation, trauma fragmentation, shame load, and clinical functioning, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, or therapeutic relationship.
