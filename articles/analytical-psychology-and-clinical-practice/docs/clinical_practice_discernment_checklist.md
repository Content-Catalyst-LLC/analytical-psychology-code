# Analytical Psychology Clinical Practice Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a treatment protocol.

## Clinical safety

- Has acute risk been assessed before symbolic interpretation?
- Are suicidality, psychosis, mania, addiction, dissociation, medical issues, or severe impairment present?
- Does the patient need medication consultation, crisis care, multidisciplinary care, or another treatment setting?
- Is the clinical frame clear enough to hold depth work?

## Symbolic work

- Can the patient use dream work, amplification, or active imagination safely?
- Is symbolic work deepening integration or increasing fragmentation?
- Is the analyst interpreting too quickly or too beautifully?
- Is the symbol connected to the patient’s actual affect, body, history, and relationship?

## Complexes and compensation

- What complex appears activated?
- What conscious one-sidedness may the symptom or dream compensate?
- Is the patient possessed by an affective pattern?
- Is the interpretation helping the patient develop relation to the complex?

## Relational field

- What is happening between patient and analyst?
- What transference and countertransference patterns are active?
- Is rupture being repaired, denied, or interpreted defensively?
- Is the analyst’s subjectivity disciplined by supervision, ethics, and reflection?

## Ethics and limits

- Are boundaries clear and reliable?
- Is Jungian language replacing clinical competence?
- Is social or cultural harm being reduced to intrapsychic symbolism?
- Is the treatment helping, or does the formulation need revision?
