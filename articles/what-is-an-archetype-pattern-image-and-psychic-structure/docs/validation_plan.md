# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or scholarly extension would require careful validation before making claims about archetypal recurrence, symbolic universality, dream interpretation, religious symbolism, cultural transmission, or psychological structure.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define motifs and symbolic clusters before analysis.
2. Separate archetypal hypotheses from cultural transmission, genre convention, developmental conditions, ecological context, ritual function, theology, and historical borrowing.
3. Use transparent corpus-selection criteria.
4. Avoid treating similarity as proof of sameness.
5. Include counterexamples and non-recurring motifs.
6. Work with historians, religious studies scholars, anthropologists, linguists, psychologists, clinicians, and cultural experts when analyzing real traditions.
7. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, private dreams, clinical material, or sacred community material without consent and ethical approval.
8. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
