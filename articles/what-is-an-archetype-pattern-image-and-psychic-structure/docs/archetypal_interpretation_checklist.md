# Archetypal Interpretation Checklist

## Description before interpretation

- What image appears?
- What happens in sequence?
- Who acts?
- What changes?
- What affect is present?
- What remains ambiguous?

## Layer distinction

- What belongs to personal biography?
- What belongs to the immediate text, dream, ritual, or narrative?
- What belongs to cultural, religious, or historical context?
- What belongs to genre convention or transmission?
- What may plausibly be archetypal?

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce living traditions to psychological examples.
- Do not treat similarity as sameness.
- Do not turn ordinary conflict into cosmic drama.
- Do not impose gender essence.
- Do not interpret private dreams without context and consent.
- Do not use archetypal language to excuse harm.
