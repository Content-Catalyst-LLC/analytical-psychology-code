# Midlife Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a treatment protocol and not life advice.

## First-half structures

- What role, persona, achievement pattern, or social identity has organized the person’s life?
- Is that structure still meaningful, or only functional?
- What did adaptation require the person to suppress, defer, or sacrifice?
- Is the person using competence to avoid grief, shadow, or symbolic life?

## Crisis dynamics

- Is there outward-inward discrepancy between visible success and inward alignment?
- Is the crisis shaped by depression, grief, trauma, burnout, illness, relational rupture, or ordinary developmental transition?
- Is symbolic intensity being confused with literal instruction?
- Is the person acting out the unlived life or reflecting on it?

## Shadow and unlived life

- What has returned from the shadow at midlife?
- Is resentment pointing to legitimate self-betrayal, or only displacement of responsibility?
- What unlived possibilities can still be lived?
- What unlived possibilities must be grieved or symbolically integrated rather than literally enacted?

## Finitude and meaning

- How has mortality, aging, parental loss, bodily change, or time awareness altered meaning?
- What values remain worth serving under limitation?
- What must be relinquished for second-half orientation to form?
- What would renewal look like without denying grief?

## Ethics and responsibility

- Does the midlife interpretation excuse harm, impulsivity, or abandonment of responsibility?
- Are social, economic, caregiving, health, and cultural constraints taken seriously?
- Does individuation deepen responsibility, or become a story of self-exemption?
- What needs professional care rather than symbolic interpretation?
