# Data Dictionary

The files in `data/raw/` are synthetic and illustrative. They do not represent real people, clinical records, psychological assessments, life outcomes, or empirical findings.

## `synthetic_midlife_transition.csv`

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Synthetic row identifier |
| `midlife_pattern` | Illustrative midlife-transition pattern |
| `time` | Synthetic time marker |
| `adaptation_strength` | Stylized role competence, outward functioning, and first-half adaptation |
| `persona_identification` | Stylized identification with role, achievement, recognition, and adaptive identity |
| `symbolic_activation` | Stylized intensity of dreams, images, creativity, spirituality, and symbolic pressure |
| `unlived_life_pressure` | Stylized pressure from excluded possibilities, shadow life, deferred desire, and unlived potential |
| `finitude_awareness` | Stylized awareness of mortality, aging, limitation, and time |
| `outward_inward_discrepancy` | Stylized gap between external success and inward alignment |
| `individuation_pressure` | Stylized pressure toward deeper reorientation and symbolic integration |
| `shadow_integration` | Stylized capacity to relate consciously to disowned aspects of personality |
| `reflective_capacity` | Stylized capacity to hold ambiguity, grief, and transition without impulsive compensation |
| `meaning_coherence` | Stylized coherence of meaning under midlife transition |
| `second_half_orientation` | Stylized orientation toward second-half values, finitude, inward truth, and individuation |
