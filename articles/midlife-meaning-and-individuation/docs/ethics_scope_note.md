# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- clinical decision-making;
- treatment recommendation;
- life prediction;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling;
- advice about whether a person should leave a job, relationship, family, institution, or life situation.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about midlife, meaning coherence, persona, shadow, unlived life, finitude, symbolic activation, reflective capacity, and individuation pressure, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, cultural context, or personal discernment.
