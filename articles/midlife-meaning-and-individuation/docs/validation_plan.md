# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about midlife development, meaning, life transition, identity, personality change, clinical outcomes, or psychological assessment.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, research measurement, and conceptual modeling.
3. Use validated measures of meaning, life satisfaction, depression, personality, identity, and adult development where appropriate.
4. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that purpose.
5. Include cultural, gendered, economic, relational, health, and institutional context.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable personal or clinical material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
8. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Distinguish symbolic interpretation from clinical assessment and life advice.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian midlife theory, diagnose crisis, recommend life choices, evaluate people, predict development, or replace clinical judgment. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
