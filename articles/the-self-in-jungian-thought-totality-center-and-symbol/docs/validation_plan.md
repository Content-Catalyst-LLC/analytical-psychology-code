# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about the Self, ego-Self relation, individuation, symbolic centers, inflation, psychological integration, therapy, diagnosis, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, qualitative research, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat model outputs as measurements of real psychic wholeness.
4. Use synthetic, public-domain, or ethically approved materials only.
5. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, statisticians, historians of psychology, religious-studies scholars, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, dream records, clinical records, or private life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
8. Compare Jungian interpretation with alternative explanations, including identity development, narrative identity, trauma integration, affect regulation, attachment, systems theory, and personality psychology.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Pair computational findings with close reading, clinical/contextual judgment, and ethical restraint.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate the Self, diagnose people, interpret real dreams, recommend treatment, claim spiritual authority, replace clinical judgment, or prove analytical psychology. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
