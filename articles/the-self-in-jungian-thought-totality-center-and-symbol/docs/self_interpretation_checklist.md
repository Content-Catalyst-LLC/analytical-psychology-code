# Self Interpretation Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, spiritual assessment, or individuation-measurement service.

## Ego-Self distinction

- Is the Self being distinguished from ego identity?
- Is the ego treated as necessary but partial?
- Does the interpretation avoid making the ego identical with wholeness?

## Symbolic center

- What center-symbol appears?
- Does it appear in a dream, active imagination, ritual image, artwork, or theoretical model?
- Does the symbol indicate achieved wholeness, or a need for orientation?
- What context, affect, and sequence surround the image?

## Integration

- What psychic domains are differentiated?
- What domains are isolated or overcentralized?
- Does integration mean ordered relation rather than fusion?
- Is conflict being honored rather than prematurely resolved?

## Inflation risk

- Is the person or group identifying with symbolic authority?
- Is a dream, symbol, or center image being treated as literal command?
- Is the Self being used to justify superiority, certainty, or avoidance of responsibility?

## Cultural and religious caution

- Are religious symbols being reduced to psychology?
- Is historical and ritual context respected?
- Is the interpretation avoiding flattening distinct traditions into one symbolic code?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not treat the Self as ego enlargement.
- Do not treat center-symbols as proof of wholeness.
- Do not use model outputs to assess real people.
- Do not turn numinous imagery into authority claims.
- Keep interpretation provisional, contextual, humble, and ethically grounded.
