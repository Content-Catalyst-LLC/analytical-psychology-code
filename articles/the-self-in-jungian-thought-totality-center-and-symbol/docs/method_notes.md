# Method Notes

This scaffold uses synthetic conceptual variables to illustrate the article’s argument. The variables are not validated psychological, clinical, therapeutic, diagnostic, spiritual, dream-interpretive, predictive, or mental-health measures and should not be interpreted as measurements of real people.

## Modeling logic

The article’s core claim is that the Jungian Self is a symbolic principle of totality and center, not ego enlargement. A simplified integration model is:

```text
symbolic totality =
  ego coherence
  + unconscious activation
  + symbolic center strength
  + relational coordination
  - disjunction
  - inflation risk
```

A simplified Self-relation model is:

```text
Self-relation =
  totality score
  + differentiation score
  + symbolic center strength
  - inflation risk
```

A simplified network model is:

```text
Self-relation emerges when differentiated clusters remain connected
without collapsing into ego dominance or fragmentation.
```

## Interpretive safeguards

- The Self is not the ego.
- A symbol of wholeness is not proof of achieved wholeness.
- Center-symbols require interpretation, not possession.
- Numinous images should not be turned into spiritual authority claims.
- Inflation risk increases when ego coherence and symbolic center strength rise without relational coordination and humility.
- Crisis, trauma, suicidality, psychosis, mania, severe dissociation, or unstable reality testing require appropriate professional care, not symbolic speculation.
