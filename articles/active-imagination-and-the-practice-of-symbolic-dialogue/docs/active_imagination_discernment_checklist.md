# Active Imagination Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a treatment protocol, crisis tool, assessment instrument, or clinical recommendation.

## Readiness and grounding

- Is there enough ego mediation to remain oriented before, during, and after the imaginal encounter?
- Can the person distinguish symbolic inner material from external fact?
- Is the material too intense for safe symbolic work right now?
- Would grounding, rest, relational support, or clinical care be more appropriate than deeper imaginal engagement?

## Symbolic encounter

- Did the image, figure, mood, or scene show some autonomy?
- Was there surprise, resistance, or symbolic movement?
- Did the ego respond rather than merely observe or obey?
- Was the encounter recorded or given form for later reflection?

## Ethical containment

- Does the encounter increase responsibility rather than bypass it?
- Is the ego refusing harmful, grandiose, or coercive demands?
- Are inner figures being treated as symbolic presences rather than unquestionable authorities?
- Is symbolic material being tested against ordinary life, relationships, and ethical judgment?

## Integration

- What changed in relation to shadow, dream material, mood, body, or projection?
- Is the material being integrated slowly rather than literalized quickly?
- Are later dreams, life events, and relationships confirming, revising, or correcting the interpretation?
- Does the practice increase humility, symbolic depth, accountability, and psychological differentiation?

## Boundaries

- Do not use active imagination as a substitute for clinical care.
- Do not use it to interpret another person's mind.
- Do not use it to justify harm.
- Do not use it as an oracle for every decision.
- Do not share intimate symbolic material publicly before it has been integrated.
