# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- mental-health evaluation;
- treatment recommendation;
- crisis intervention;
- clinical decision-making;
- dream interpretation for real people;
- interpretation of private journals or inner images;
- advice about relationships, careers, religion, family, or major life decisions;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about ego mediation, imaginal activation, symbolic relation, reflective response, ethical containment, destabilization risk, inflation risk, and integration, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, cultural context, or personal discernment.
