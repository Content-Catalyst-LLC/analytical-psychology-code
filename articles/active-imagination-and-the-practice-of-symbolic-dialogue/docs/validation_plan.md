# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about active imagination, symbolic dialogue, psychotherapy, creativity, dream work, integration, diagnosis, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, qualitative research, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat symbolic-dialogue outputs as mental-health measures or therapeutic recommendations.
4. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, statisticians, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
5. Protect privacy and never use identifiable clinical, dream, journal, therapy, religious, family, workplace, or life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
6. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
7. Include cultural, religious, gendered, socioeconomic, institutional, and historical context.
8. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that specific purpose.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate active imagination, diagnose people, recommend treatment, interpret dreams, assess inner images, evaluate mental health, predict development, or replace clinical judgment. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
