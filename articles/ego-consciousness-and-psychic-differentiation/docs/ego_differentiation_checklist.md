# Ego Differentiation Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment protocol, personality assessment, or individuation-measurement service.

## Ego clarity

- Is the ego distinguished from the whole psyche?
- Is ego strength distinguished from ego inflation?
- Is consciousness treated as necessary but partial?
- Does the interpretation avoid ego annihilation language?

## Differentiation

- What distinctions are being formed: self/other, inner/outer, image/fact, role/personhood, thought/feeling?
- Is differentiation increasing responsibility or merely increasing one-sidedness?
- Which function or domain is dominant?
- Which function or domain may remain inferior or underdeveloped?

## Persona and shadow

- Is the ego identified with a social role?
- What does the persona exclude?
- What shadow material may be forming around the preferred self-image?
- Is projection being distinguished from external fact?

## Inflation and rigidity

- Is symbolic or archetypal material being claimed by the ego?
- Is a public role or moral image becoming total identity?
- Is certainty replacing reflection?
- Is humility present?

## Responsible conclusion

- Do not treat model outputs as real assessments.
- Do not use ego-strength language to rank people.
- Do not turn typology into fixed identity.
- Do not use symbolic analysis where clinical safety or grounding is needed.
- Keep interpretation provisional, contextual, humble, and ethically grounded.
