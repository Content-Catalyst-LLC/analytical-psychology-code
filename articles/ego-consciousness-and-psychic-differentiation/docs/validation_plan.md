# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about ego development, consciousness, psychic differentiation, typological balance, ego strength, ego inflation, shadow pressure, therapy, diagnosis, personality, or clinical outcomes.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, clinical practice, qualitative research, and conceptual modeling.
3. Do not treat model outputs as measurements of real ego strength or individuation.
4. Use synthetic, public-domain, or ethically approved materials only.
5. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, statisticians, historians of psychology, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable therapy notes, clinical records, dream records, or private life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include trauma-informed safeguards and clear stopping conditions.
8. Compare Jungian interpretation with alternative explanations, including cognitive development, self-regulation, identity development, affect regulation, personality psychology, psychodynamic theory, attachment, and trauma research.
9. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.
10. Pair computational findings with close reading, clinical/contextual judgment, and ethical restraint.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not diagnose people, interpret real dreams, recommend treatment, assess personality, replace clinical judgment, or prove analytical psychology. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
