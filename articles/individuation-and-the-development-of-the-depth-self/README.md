# Individuation and the Development of the Depth Self

This companion scaffold supports the article **“Individuation and the Development of the Depth Self”** in the Analytical Psychology, Symbolism & the Depth Mind knowledge series.

The repository materials translate the article’s argument into reproducible, synthetic-data workflows. The goal is not to diagnose people, predict life choices, evaluate individuals, recommend therapy, or reduce individuation to a formula. The goal is to demonstrate how ego coherence, persona identification, shadow acknowledgment, complex activation, symbolic relation, reflective capacity, body awareness, ethical accountability, relational life, one-sidedness, compensation pressure, depth-self integration, and individuation potential can be represented transparently in conceptual modeling.

## Repository purpose

This article argues that individuation is not individualism, self-branding, ego perfection, or private authenticity rhetoric. It is the long process by which the ego comes into more truthful relation with the unconscious, shadow, complexes, symbolic life, ethical responsibility, and the Self as a wider psychic center. The depth self is not discovered as a preformed essence; it develops through differentiation, symbolic encounter, confrontation with one-sidedness, and integration under tension.

The code examples extend that argument through:

- synthetic individuation and depth-self data;
- individuation-score and one-sidedness simulation;
- persona, shadow, complex, and symbolic-center modeling;
- compensation-pressure and depth-self integration workflows;
- dynamic individuation network scripts;
- reproducible tables and figures;
- multi-language computational scaffolding;
- documented responsible-use limits.

## Responsible-use statement

These materials are for synthetic-data research, methods demonstration, conceptual modeling, symbolic-process analysis, institutional learning, and reproducible workflows. They are not intended for diagnosis, therapy, psychological assessment, clinical decision-making, life prediction, employment screening, workplace surveillance, individual performance management, or individual evaluation.

## Suggested workflow

1. Review `article-metadata.yml` for article context.
2. Inspect the synthetic data in `data/raw/synthetic_individuation_depth_self.csv`.
3. Run the R workflow in `r/simulate_individuation_depth_self.R`.
4. Run the Python workflow in `python/depth_self_network.py`.
5. Use `sql/schema.sql` to inspect the individuation schema.
6. Treat all outputs as illustrative and synthetic, not empirical findings, assessment scores, or clinical claims.
