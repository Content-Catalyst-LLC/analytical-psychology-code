# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or clinical extension would require careful validation before making any claim about individuation, symbolic development, shadow work, personality change, psychotherapy outcomes, diagnosis, or psychological assessment.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define each construct clearly.
2. Separate interpretive theory, research measurement, and conceptual modeling.
3. Use validated personality, meaning, identity, attachment, symptom, psychotherapy-process, and well-being measures where appropriate.
4. Work with qualified clinicians, research psychologists, statisticians, and ethics review processes for human-subjects research.
5. Avoid individual-level scoring unless a validated instrument exists for that purpose.
6. Protect privacy and never use identifiable clinical, personal, dream, therapy, or life-history material without consent and ethical approval.
7. Include cultural, gendered, socioeconomic, institutional, religious, and historical context.
8. Include disconfirming cases and alternative explanations.
9. Document uncertainty and avoid overclaiming.
10. Treat model outputs as hypotheses or demonstrations, not psychological truth.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate Jungian individuation theory, diagnose psychological states, recommend treatment, assess maturity, predict development, or replace clinical judgment. It is an educational and conceptual modeling scaffold.
