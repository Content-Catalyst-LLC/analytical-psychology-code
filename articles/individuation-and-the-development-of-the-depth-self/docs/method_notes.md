# Method Notes

This scaffold uses synthetic conceptual variables to illustrate the article’s argument. The variables are not validated psychological, clinical, therapeutic, diagnostic, personality-assessment, or life-outcome measures and should not be interpreted as measurements of real people, therapy processes, or psychological development.

## Modeling logic

The article’s core claim is that individuation is the development of a less divided relation among ego, unconscious life, shadow, complexes, symbolic relation, body, ethics, relationship, and the Self as a wider orienting center.

A simplified individuation model is:

```text
individuation potential =
  ego coherence
  + shadow acknowledgment
  + symbolic relation
  + reflective capacity
  + ethical accountability
  + body awareness
  + relational life
  - persona identification
  - unresolved complex activation
  - destructive one-sidedness
```

A simplified one-sidedness model is:

```text
one-sidedness =
  (ego coherence - shadow acknowledgment)^2
  + (symbolic relation - complex activation)^2
  + (persona identification - reflective capacity)^2
```

A simplified compensation-pressure model is:

```text
compensation pressure =
  one-sidedness
  + unresolved complex activation
  - reflective capacity
```

A simplified depth-self-integration model is:

```text
depth-self integration =
  ego coherence
  + shadow acknowledgment
  + symbolic relation
  + reflective capacity
  + body awareness
  + ethical accountability
  + relational life
  - complex activation
  - one-sidedness
```

## Limits

These examples are conceptual demonstrations. They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- personality assessment;
- treatment recommendation;
- clinical decision-making;
- life prediction;
- employment screening;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual scoring;
- individual evaluation;
- advice about relationships, careers, religion, or life choices;
- claims about empirical effectiveness;
- proof or disproof of Jungian individuation theory.

## Recommended use

Use these files to demonstrate reproducible research structure, transparent assumptions, synthetic simulation, dynamic individuation-network modeling, and conceptual-method documentation in scholarly writing.
