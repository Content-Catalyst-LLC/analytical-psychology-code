# Individuation Discernment Checklist

Use this checklist for conceptual and editorial review. It is not a treatment protocol, assessment tool, or life-advice instrument.

## Ego and stabilization

- Is the ego strong enough to engage unconscious material without collapse or inflation?
- Is the person grounded in reality, relationship, body, and responsibility?
- Is symbolic work appropriate, or is stabilization the first need?
- Are trauma, dissociation, psychosis, mania, severe depression, or crisis risk being handled with appropriate clinical care?

## Persona and shadow

- What role, image, moral identity, or adaptive mask has become overidentified?
- What shadow material is returning through projection, dream, affect, envy, resentment, or conflict?
- Is shadow being integrated consciously or acted out?
- Is self-knowledge increasing responsibility rather than excusing harm?

## Complexes and symbols

- What complex appears to be active?
- Is the complex being treated as a living pattern rather than a fixed identity?
- Are symbols being held with context, humility, and restraint?
- Is symbolic intensity being mistaken for literal instruction?

## Self and inflation

- Is the ego relating to the Self, or identifying with it?
- Are numinous experiences deepening humility and accountability?
- Is the person claiming special exemption, destiny, or superiority?
- Is the symbolic center being grounded in ordinary life?

## Ethics and community

- Does individuation deepen relationship, responsibility, and truthful participation?
- Are cultural, historical, gendered, racialized, institutional, and social conditions being considered?
- Is private authenticity being confused with ethical development?
- Does the work reduce projection and increase accountability?
