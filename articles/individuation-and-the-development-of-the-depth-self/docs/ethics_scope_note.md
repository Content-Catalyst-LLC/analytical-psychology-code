# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- personality assessment;
- clinical decision-making;
- treatment recommendation;
- life prediction;
- advice about relationships, careers, religion, family, or major life decisions;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- individual performance management;
- individual psychological profiling.

Conceptual modeling can clarify assumptions about individuation, ego coherence, persona identification, shadow acknowledgment, complex activation, symbolic relation, reflective capacity, body awareness, ethical accountability, relational life, one-sidedness, compensation pressure, depth-self integration, and relation to the Self, but it cannot replace clinical judgment, ethical care, empirical validation, cultural context, or personal discernment.
