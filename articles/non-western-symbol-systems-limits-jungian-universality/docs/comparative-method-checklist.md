# Comparative Method Checklist

Use this checklist before making a Jungian or archetypal comparison across traditions.

## Before comparison

- What is the specific symbol, motif, ritual, image, text, or practice?
- Which tradition, language, region, and historical period are involved?
- Is the material public, restricted, initiatory, sacred, or community-governed?
- What does the tradition itself say the symbol is?
- Who has authority to interpret it?

## During comparison

- Is the resemblance visual, narrative, ritual, theological, social, ecological, or psychological?
- Does the symbol perform the same function across contexts?
- What differences matter?
- Are there disconfirming examples?
- Is the Jungian category clarifying or replacing local meaning?

## After comparison

- Have alternative explanations been considered?
- Has the distinction between recurrence and identity been preserved?
- Is the claim thin and modest, or thick and universalizing?
- Is the comparison dialogical or extractive?
- What might be erased by the interpretation?
