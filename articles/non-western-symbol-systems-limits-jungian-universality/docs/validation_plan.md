# Validation Plan

This scaffold is conceptual and synthetic. A serious empirical or scholarly extension would require careful validation, expert consultation, and community accountability before making any claim about real symbolic systems.

## Possible validation steps

1. Define the symbol, tradition, region, language, and historical period precisely.
2. Distinguish formal resemblance from functional equivalence.
3. Consult primary sources and tradition-specific scholarship.
4. Work with scholars, practitioners, elders, or community authorities where appropriate.
5. Respect restricted, sacred, initiatory, or community-governed knowledge.
6. Include disconfirming examples and divergent functions.
7. Document translation uncertainty.
8. Avoid treating resemblance as proof of universal psychic structure.
9. Separate psychological interpretation from theological, ritual, legal, ecological, or communal meaning.
10. Clearly state the limits of any Jungian reading.

## Non-goals

This scaffold does not validate archetypes, prove the collective unconscious, evaluate religions, rank cultures, interpret sacred systems, or authorize the use of restricted cultural material.
