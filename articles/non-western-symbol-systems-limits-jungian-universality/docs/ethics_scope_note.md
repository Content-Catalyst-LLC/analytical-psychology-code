# Ethics and Scope Note

The materials in this folder are designed for scholarly demonstration and reproducible conceptual modeling.

They should not be used for:

- diagnosis;
- therapy;
- psychological assessment;
- employment selection;
- hiring;
- promotion;
- compensation decisions;
- discipline;
- termination;
- workplace surveillance;
- cultural ranking;
- religious evaluation;
- sacred-symbol extraction;
- individual psychological profiling.

Comparative symbolic modeling can clarify assumptions about recurrence, specificity, universality, and flattening risk, but it cannot replace philology, anthropology, theology, area studies, Indigenous studies, African studies, Islamic studies, Asian studies, ritual studies, oral-history methods, decolonial critique, community accountability, or ethical interpretation.
